# business-rule-mapping
Separate documented requirements, observed behavior and hypotheses. Output rule_id, process, condition, action, exceptions, responsible role, system, evidence doc_id/version/section, confidence and review status. Flag conflicting revisions and missing evidence. Do not infer actual bottleneck timing from documents; propose event data or manual sampling. No rule becomes approved without human owner.
